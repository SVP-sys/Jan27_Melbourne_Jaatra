Good to know

A few things that will make the nights run smoothly.

Book the small bars

Whisky and Alement, Beneath Driver Lane, The Florence and Eau de Vie are small. Call ahead for a table for six.

Friday is different

Whisky and Alement and Beneath Driver Lane are closed on Fridays, so Whisky Den covers the whisky that night.

Start early

The rooftops fill up from 4pm, and Fleet closes at midnight on Saturday. Go there first.

Check the hours

Hours come from Google listings and can change. Recheck a day or two before you go.
